### 4. MCP-ify All Services (Unified 5-Tool Architecture)

Existing backend services ko scratch se rewrite karne ki zaroorat nahi hai; hum existing controllers and drivers ko **5 Standard MCP Tools** ke form mein wrap karenge:

1. **`db_tool`**: Actions `[query, schema, connections, sync_er]` (Reuses `dbRoutes.ts`, `PgDriver`, `MongoDriver`, `RedisDriver`, `sql.js`).
2. **`diagram_tool`**: Actions `[create, update, delete, list, get, generate_ai]` (Reuses `diagramRoutes.ts`, Excalidraw store).
3. **`screen_tool`**: Actions `[generate_stitch, modify_element, export_fig]` (Reuses `screenRoutes.ts`, OpenPencil core, `figExporter.ts`).
4. **`git_tool`**: Actions `[status, branches, commits, diff, sync]` (Reuses `gitRoutes.ts`, `simple-git`).
5. **`qa_tool`**: Actions `[audit_schema, apply_remediation, ast_safety, run_tests]` (Reuses `qaDiagnosticsService.ts`, `qaAstSafetyService.ts`, `qaTestRunnerService.ts`).

Yeh 5 tools **Central `/chat` page** aur **In-Page AI buttons** dono se identically call honge.
