The design of the 3-panel Context Management System / Vibe Coding Cockpit has been generated natively in OpenPencil using our built-in synthesizer and saved directly to the ui/ directory.

Files generated:
1. ui/dark_mode_3_panel_context_management_system.fig & .json
2. ui/vibe_coding_project_context_hub_3_panel_layout.fig & .json

To view and inspect the design in OpenPencil:
1. Open http://localhost:5173/screens in your browser.
2. Click "Open from Project Root (ui/)".
3. Select dark_mode_3_panel_context_management_system.fig or vibe_coding_project_context_hub_3_panel_layout.fig.
