## Summary for Joint Decision

- **Core to Retain (Items 1–10):** 10 verified, production-ready modules forming the coherent foundation of AI Manager.
- **Candidates for Consolidation (Item 11):** Merge `ProjectDetailPage.tsx` into the 3-panel Context Cockpit to eliminate duplicate views.
- **Candidates for Safe Deletion (Items 12–14):**
  - Delete `OnboardingPage.tsx`.
  - Delete 11 abandoned `*Screen.tsx` components in `src/components/`.
  - Delete unused `server/modules.ts` and `server/contextRoutes.ts`.
